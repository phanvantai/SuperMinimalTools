SuperMinimalTools 0.1.3

  • Vietnamese Typing. Turn it on from the dropdown to type Vietnamese with Telex in every app (aa → â, aw → ă, ow → ơ, uw → ư, dd → đ, s f r x j for the tones). Words that can't be Vietnamese, like "string" or "google", stay as typed. Press ⌃⇧ to switch between Vietnamese and English; each app remembers its own mode, and the menu bar shows VI or EN. Needs the Accessibility permission. Quit other Vietnamese input apps first.
