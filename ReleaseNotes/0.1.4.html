SuperMinimalTools 0.1.4

  • Vietnamese Typing types like PHTV. Type the d anywhere in a word (did → đi), and abbreviations keep it (ddc → đc). ưo becomes ươ as soon as more letters follow (dduwong → đương). Repeating a letter stretches the word (đẹpppp, khôngggg) instead of undoing its marks.
  • Backspace works better: it keeps the marks when you delete a stray letter (việtn → việt), and deleting the space after a word lets you keep editing that word (viet, space, backspace, j → việt).
