SuperMinimalTools 0.1.2

  • Clipboard History. Turn it on from the dropdown and SuperMinimalTools remembers the last 200 pieces of text you copy. Press ⌃⌘V anywhere for a quick picker: type to search, use the arrow keys, press Return to copy an item back, then ⌘V to paste. Text that password managers mark as concealed is never recorded, the history stays on your Mac, and turning the feature off deletes it.
  • Automatic updates. The app now checks for new versions once a day and asks before installing. Use Check for Updates… in the dropdown any time, or turn automatic checks off.
  • Builds are now signed and notarized, so macOS opens them without a Gatekeeper warning.
